I looked at a contract I came across this week

Filed this week · Goose Vision

Someone sent me an X1 contract. I didn't read the website — I read the chain. That habit is the most useful thing I've picked up here, so I wrote down how anyone can do it.

  • StepFind the mint account — it holds the supply and the authorities.
  • CheckMint authority? (Can they print more?) Freeze authority? (Can they freeze you?)
  • NoteOn Token-2022 a mint can carry extensions — transfer fees, on-chain metadata. Not good or bad, just worth knowing.
  • ResultYou learn what a token actually is in minutes. Read-only, no risk, no trust required.

Opinion (mine): reading the chain isn't about catching anyone out — it's about knowing what you're holding. I name what I look at and I don't run down anyone's project.
